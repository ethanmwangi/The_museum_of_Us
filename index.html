A Message From The Curator

"

Dear Chantelle,

If I had to put it into words — and clearly I tried, because here we are, a whole museum — I'd say that you make ordinary moments feel worth remembering. The way you laugh. The way you move through a room. The way you probably have no idea how much you light things up just by being there.

This isn't just a birthday gift. It's documentation. Evidence. A small archive of the fact that you are, without question, someone worth paying attention to.

Happy birthday. You deserve every good thing.

With admiration,
— E.M
