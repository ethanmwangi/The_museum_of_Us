 The Museum of Chantelle

> *A private exhibition. Curated with admiration · Est. 2026*

A personal birthday gift built as an interactive museum website — dedicated to someone worth documenting properly.

---

Features

- **Gallery** — A curated collection of exhibits with scroll-in animations and hover effects
- **Lightbox** — Click any photo to expand it fullscreen
- **Birthday Countdown** — Live countdown ticking down to April 5th
- **Curator's Note** — A personal message from the curator
- **Sign the Wall** — Visitors can doodle on a canvas wall with custom colours and brush sizes
- **Guestbook** — Leave a birthday wish that gets saved to the page
- **Confetti Explosion** — A button that triggers confetti and redirects to a dedicated birthday page
- **Floating Petals** — Rose and gold petals drift across the entire site
